# Goals

## Purpose

Provide a durable control plane for coordinating independent AI-assisted projects.

## Desired outcomes

- Route work to the correct project.
- Avoid duplicate workers.
- Keep context and token usage bounded.
- Preserve decisions, blockers, handoffs, and verified results.
- Maintain clear approval and project-boundary controls.

## Non-goals

- Combining all project files into one repository.
- Bypassing permissions or sandbox boundaries.
- Automatically making destructive or external changes without appropriate authorization.
- Storing credentials, secrets, or private identifiers.
